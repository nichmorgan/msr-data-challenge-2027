\section{Extended Pipeline}

Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline-extended} expands Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline-simple} from Section~\ref{sec:future}.
Each major stage keeps a top-to-bottom order.
A step that relies on a model is followed by a sample check, then either a change of labels or cutoff or a manual review of gaps, and then a merge before the next stage.
